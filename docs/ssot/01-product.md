# Product
Status: Draft | Last updated: 2026-11-24

OS is a standalone CRM and AI business intelligence application for PROSPERA Digital Solutions, a digital agency in Mumbai, India. It manages the complete business sales lifecycle in a single product.

## In scope
- Enquiries and leads; client and contact management.
- Sales pipeline; activities and follow-ups.
- Quotations and orders; payments and expenses.
- Revenue and business analytics.
- AI business analysis and sales recommendations.
- Instagram and WhatsApp lead integrations.
- Approved Phase 3A scope revision (2026-11-24): Phase 3 leads are entered manually in OS. Phase 8 adds Meta ads lead intake alongside Instagram and WhatsApp; no provider intake is implemented in Phase 3A.
- Customization within this same product when needed.

## Explicitly out of scope
- Multi-tenancy, tenant/organization IDs and tenant switching.
- Per-client deployments.
- SaaS subscription and billing infrastructure. Business payments remain in scope.
- Business tables or Phase 2 implementation during Phase 1.

## Open questions
- Phase 2 approves equal access for every authenticated user with no roles/permissions, and invite-only dashboard-created accounts. Lifecycle rules and module acceptance criteria remain open.
